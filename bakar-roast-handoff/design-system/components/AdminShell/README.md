The admin frame: a sidebar that becomes a drawer on phone, with the pending count, the signed-in name and sign out.

- Sidebar is `ink-soft`, 220px on laptop. On phone it is a drawer opened from a top bar that shows the page title and the pending badge.
- Sections are grouped so more can join: Catering now (Requests, Calendar, Packages, Gallery and testimonials, Settings). Orders, Menu, Batches and Payments from The Bun Theory arrive later as a second group. Do not show later items until they exist; the preview shows two only to prove the room.
- Current item: `ink-raised` fill, cream text and an `ember` bar on the left. Links are 44px tall.
- The pending badge is `ember` with `ink-base` digits and hides at zero.
- Admin is plainer and denser than the public site: no grain, no glows, no parallax, no stamp or marquee, no display headlines. Page titles use `admin-title`. Fades and short slides only.
- Login sits outside the shell: wordmark, email, password, one `ember` button, and an error Banner reading "That email or password is not right."
- Settings hold the four booking numbers and the notification email. Everything customers read about notice, window, hold and cutoff comes from here.
