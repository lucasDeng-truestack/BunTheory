Numbered rows with a huge title and a round arrow, for the occasions we cater.

- Occasions: House gatherings, Celebrations, Community and church, Small corporate. Each row links into booking with that occasion preselected.
- Hairlines are `ink-line` and decorative; the whole row is the link, at least 44px tall.
- Title uses `display-lg` on desktop and scales down to 36px on phone. On hover the title turns `ember` and the arrow circle fills.
- You provide the titles, the destination, and one photo per row for the hover effect.
- Motion (fine pointers only): a photo follows the cursor, swaps per row and tilts with pointer speed. Touch devices get the plain list.
