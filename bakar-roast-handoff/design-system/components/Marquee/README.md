Two crossed, tilted ribbons of looping words, one ember and one cream, both with `ink-base` text.

- Sits once per page between the hero and the intro. Words: "Wood fire", "Apple-wood smoke", "Cooked fresh", "Served warm".
- Ribbons are 56px tall, tilted 3 degrees in opposite directions, and bleed past both page edges.
- Motion: loops forever, speeds up with scroll speed, reverses when the scroll direction reverses, and the two ribbons change tilt as they pass. Under reduced motion the ribbons sit still.
- The repeated words are decoration: put one readable `aria-label` on the wrapper and hide the tracks.
- Public pages only.
