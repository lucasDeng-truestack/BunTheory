Numbered step cards that step down like a staircase, for "Four steps to a full table."

- The four steps are fixed: Pick a date. Choose a package. We call you. We cook. Each gets one short line under it.
- Cards are `ink-raised` on an `ink-base` band (or `ink-base` cards on a raised band), `radius-md`, number in `ember`.
- Desktop: four across, each 32px lower than the one before. Phone: one column, no stagger.
- Use an ordered list. You provide the title and one line per step.
- Motion: cards rise in from further down the later they are in the row.
